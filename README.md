## 📖 About GameZone

**GameZone** is an offline Android gaming application that includes more than 15 fun and challenging games.

Players can enjoy action, racing, puzzle, memory, maths and multiplayer games without an internet connection.

---

## ✨ General Features

- 🎮 All games work completely offline
- 🌟 Animated splash screen with particle effects
- 🕹️ Modern game-selection menu with neon cards
- ✨ Floating dots and glowing background animations
- 🏆 High-score and best-time tracking
- 🎚️ Multiple difficulty levels
- ⏸️ Pause, resume and restart controls
- 👥 Two-player and computer modes

---

## 🕹️ Available Games

### ♟️ ChessX
Play against another player or the computer with Easy, Medium and Hard AI.

Includes legal move highlighting, pawn promotion, checkmate detection, themes, timer and scoring.

### 🚀 SpaceBlast
Control a spaceship, destroy aliens, defeat bosses and avoid asteroids.

Includes four difficulty levels, power-ups, score boosts, pause and high-score tracking.

### 🍎 Fruit Slicer
Swipe to slice fruits, create combos and avoid bombs.

Includes special fruits, four difficulty levels, three lives and colourful effects.

### 🃏 Card Pairs
Find matching cards before the timer ends.

Includes three board sizes, 26 card designs, hints, animations and saved best scores.

### 🏎️ Speed Racer
Move between lanes, avoid vehicles and increase your score.

Includes five car colours, four difficulty levels, three lives and level progression.

### 🐦 Fly Birdie
Tap to fly through pipes and earn points.

The game becomes faster and more difficult as the score increases.

### 🔢 Number Flash
Memorise numbers and enter them in the correct order.

Includes three difficulty levels, timers, streaks and automatic answer checking.

### 🐍 Hungry Snake
Control the snake using buttons, swipe or tilt controls.

Includes eight themes, food scoring, collision detection and high-score tracking.

### ⭕ Tic Tac Toe
Play with a friend or challenge the computer.

Includes Easy, Medium and Hard AI, custom symbols, move timer and winning animations.

### 📝 Word Puzzle
Find hidden words by swiping across the letter grid.

Includes three grid sizes, 50+ words, hints and saved best completion times.

### 📐 Math Master
Solve addition, subtraction, multiplication and division questions.

Includes three difficulty levels, a two-minute timer and high-score tracking.

### 🎈 Balloon Blast
Pop balloons before they disappear from the screen.

Includes three difficulty levels, colourful effects, speed bonuses and saved high scores.

### 🏆 Ball Under Cup
Watch the cups shuffle and find the hidden ball.

Includes three difficulty levels, score streaks, timer and smooth cup animations.

### ✊ Rock Paper Scissors
Play against the computer or another player.

Includes three AI levels, first-to-three scoring, animations and saved statistics.
